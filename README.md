## 🕹️ About

Gunman Contracts - Stand Alone is a cinematic single-player first-person shooter for Windows, playable in VR and non-VR modes. You play as a professional hitman taking on criminal forces through narrated contracts, using firearms and close-quarters weapons in action-movie-inspired missions. The game is in development and planned for Early Access.

Designed for players who enjoy cinematic single-player shooters, intense gunplay, VR action, and replayable contract-based challenges.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Contract Missions** — Complete narrated single-player operations against organized criminal forces.
- 🔫 **Customizable Arsenal** — Use and customize pistols, rifles, shotguns, knives, and a bow.
- 🥽 **VR and Flat Play** — Play the full shooter experience in PC VR or traditional non-VR mode.
- ⚔️ **Challenge Modes** — Replay contracts through Takedown and Last Stand challenges.
- 🏢 **Gunman's Base** — Train, test weapons, and complete skill challenges between contracts.
- 🏆 **Leaderboards** — Compete for high scores across contracts, challenges, and training activities.
- 🎵 **Custom Music** — Play supported music files through the Gunman's in-game smartphone.
